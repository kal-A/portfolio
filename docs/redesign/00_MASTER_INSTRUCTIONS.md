# Informatica, Hera Fertility, and PathPeer - Master Portfolio Instructions

## Purpose

Build or substantially improve the dedicated portfolio pages for Hera Fertility, PathPeer, and Informatica.

Treat `PORTFOLIO_CASE_STUDY_SYSTEM.md` as the source of truth. Use the existing ForceN and Greenhouse pages as the benchmark for narrative clarity, design personality, section pacing, interaction quality, and polish.

Do not copy their layouts mechanically. Each experience needs its own visual identity while clearly belonging to the same portfolio.

## What these pages must communicate

The pages should sell Kamal's capability through evidence, decisions, and outcomes. A visitor should leave with a clear sense that he can:

- Understand a product and the wider system around it
- Turn ambiguous problems into structured plans
- Research user behaviour and translate findings into product decisions
- Plan features, roadmaps, and execution
- Work across design, product, analytics, no-code, and technical teams
- Create practical UX concepts and communicate them clearly
- Connect product work to business value, including time saved, cost avoided, conversion, retention, operational efficiency, or revenue opportunity when supported

Use strong, concise diction. Explain why the work mattered, not just what Kamal touched.

## Evidence and accuracy

Never invent metrics, revenue, cost savings, team sizes, user quotes, research sessions, launch status, adoption, technical implementation, responsibilities, or artifacts.

If a metric exists in the current portfolio or resume, verify its source before treating it as fact.

If business value is clear but not numerically documented, explain the mechanism qualitatively. Examples include reducing manual work, shortening decision cycles, reducing third-party dependency, improving funnel clarity, or helping prioritize higher-value features.

If a fact is uncertain, add it to an internal `NEEDS_INPUT` list and keep it off the public page.

Use:
- `I` for Kamal's direct work
- `we` for shared team work
- `reconstructed` labels for visuals rebuilt after the fact

## Writing rules

- No em dashes
- Short paragraphs
- Strong section titles
- Specific verbs
- Avoid generic phrases such as "leveraged", "helped drive", or "worked cross-functionally" without explaining how
- Prefer decisions, reasoning, and consequences over adjective-heavy copy
- Do not repeat the same accomplishment throughout the page
- Keep tools contextual to the work

## Shared visual system

Maintain the portfolio's existing editorial language:

- Large serif display headings
- Strong section numbering
- Thin dark borders and offset shadows
- Subtle gradients and tonal background changes
- Controlled whitespace
- Clear hierarchy
- Interactive cards where useful
- Responsive layouts
- Useful hover and focus states
- Accessible contrast
- Reduced-motion support

Each page should feel alive and designed, not like a flat template.

## Before editing

For each page:

1. Inspect the current route, content, components, and styling.
2. Read all available source notes and artifacts.
3. Create a brief internal plan with narrative spine, hiring signal, sections, visuals, verified outcomes, and missing facts.
4. Reuse shared portfolio components where useful without sacrificing the experience's personality.
5. Continue with supported content even when optional facts are missing.

Do not pause just to ask for missing optional details. Log them in `NEEDS_INPUT` and keep moving.
