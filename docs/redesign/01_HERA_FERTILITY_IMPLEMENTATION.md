# Hera Fertility - Portfolio Implementation Instructions

## Positioning

Treat Hera as a Product / UX case study with strong product-management support.

The page should show Kamal moving between user research, product planning, UX design, analytics, feature definition, and implementation support. The central impression should be that he could investigate a product problem, make sense of the evidence, design a better experience, and create a practical plan for getting it built.

Narrative spine:

> Turning complex fertility and financing journeys into clearer digital product flows, then using research and analytics to decide what should be built next.

Do not reduce Hera to a wireframe gallery.

## Handwritten work

The sketches are evidence of actual thinking. Do not hide them.

For the strongest flows:

1. Show the original sketch or research excerpt.
2. Create a polished, portfolio-safe reconstruction beside it.
3. Translate the sketch into a realistic webpage or product screen showing how it would work in practice.
4. Label the polished screen `reconstructed concept` if it was not the shipped UI.
5. Preserve the original logic rather than inventing a prettier but different product.

Use a "sketch to product" interaction, paired panel, or click-through sequence where useful.

## Suggested page structure

### Hero
Use a product-led headline, concise summary, role, scope, tools, and strongest verified outcome.

Visually combine a polished financing screen, an original handwritten artifact, and one clinic discovery or payment-plan concept.

### 01. The product problem
Explain the friction Hera was trying to remove, especially the complexity of financing, treatment discovery, and information-heavy fertility journeys.

### 02. Designing the financing flow
Use the financial application wireframes and roadmap.

Explain what users needed to provide, what information had to remain visible, how the prequalification flow was structured, and how the work moved from concept toward implementation planning.

### 03. Making payment options easier to understand
Use the payment-plan research.

Show how Kamal compared package and subscription patterns from fertility and non-fertility products, then translated those patterns into a clearer Hera concept.

The references are design-pattern research, not necessarily direct competitors.

### 04. Finding care nearby
Use `Near me design (1).pdf`.

Show search and filtering, services, price range, distance, operating days and hours, clinic cards, map integration, clinic details, and related listings.

Show the layout iterations and what each was trying to solve.

### 05. Using behaviour to decide what to improve
Use the analytics notes and supporting repo evidence.

Explain how Google Analytics, Looker Studio, and Hotjar were used to inspect traffic, channel mix, page behaviour, engagement, drop-off, clicks, session patterns, and geographic patterns where supported.

Do not claim causation from analytics alone.

Use a visual like:

`Behaviour signal -> Interpretation -> Product question -> Feature or UX action -> Measurement`

### 06. Turning ideas into a roadmap
Use the roadmap artifact.

Explain sequencing, staging or deployment planning, QA, documentation, product requirements, dependencies, and launch preparation where supported.

This section should demonstrate PM competency, not merely show a roadmap image.

### 07. Outcomes
Prioritize business-relevant value.

Possible categories, only when supported:
- lower onboarding drop-off
- higher retention or completion
- reduced reliance on external loan-servicing workflows
- faster iteration through clearer requirements
- better feature prioritization from behavioural evidence
- reduced ambiguity for implementation
- clearer paths to financing and clinic discovery

Use exact figures only when verified.

### 08. Reflection
Keep concise. Strong themes include:
- Analytics is most useful when it changes a product decision.
- Early sketches are valuable when they make assumptions visible.
- Financing UX needs to balance clarity, trust, and information density.
- Product planning is stronger when design, data, and implementation constraints are considered together.

## Visual direction

Hera should feel calm, polished, humane, and product-focused.

Use sky and cauliflower blues, deeper blue accents, warm ivory and beige neutrals, subtle gradients, spacious editorial layouts, framed product screens, and clear transitions from raw notes to polished concepts.

Do not make the page sterile or overly medical.

## Metrics rule

If the current page or resume claims 2x retention, onboarding drop-off cut in half, or another numerical improvement, verify the baseline, period, definition, and source before rendering it.

If verification is unavailable, keep the stronger business story and omit the number.
